The full cycle

Four weeks, one deliverable a week

Design and engineering run in parallel, so nothing waits on a handoff. Every week ends with something you can click.

  1. Week 1

    Discover & define

    Workshops, user journeys and competitor teardown. We agree on the core use case, the scope and the one number that says the MVP worked.

    Product spec, architecture, success metric
  2. Week 2

    Design & foundations

    Wireframes become a clickable prototype and a UI kit. In parallel: repository, data model, auth, CI/CD and cloud environments.

    Clickable prototype, running skeleton app
  3. Week 3

    Build & integrate

    Core features end to end, AI capabilities and third-party integrations — payments, email, CRM, your internal APIs — with an evaluation set for every AI feature.

    Feature-complete staging build
  4. Week 4

    Test & launch

    QA, security and performance checks, analytics and monitoring. We ship to production, onboard your first users and hand over everything.

    Live product, handover, v2 roadmap
